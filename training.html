Security Awareness for Employees

Stay informed about the latest security best practices and protect yourself, your colleagues, and our customers from security threats.

Security Best Practices

  • Lock your computer when stepping away from your desk
  • Keep your workspace clean and free of sensitive documents
  • Use company-approved software and applications only
  • Never share your login credentials with anyone
  • Report lost or stolen devices immediately
  • Use VPN when accessing company systems remotely

Phishing Awareness

  • Be suspicious of unexpected emails, especially those requesting urgent action
  • Verify sender email addresses carefully before clicking links
  • Never provide passwords or sensitive information via email
  • Hover over links to preview URLs before clicking
  • Report suspicious emails to IT Security immediately
  • When in doubt, contact the sender through a known channel

Password Guidelines

  • Use passwords with at least 12 characters
  • Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
  • Never reuse passwords across different accounts
  • Change passwords every 90 days
  • Use a password manager to store complex passwords securely
  • Enable multi-factor authentication wherever possible

Quick Security Checklist

  • Is your operating system up to date?
  • Is antivirus software active and current?
  • Have you enabled automatic security updates?
  • Are you using two-factor authentication?
  • Have you completed your annual security training?
  • Do you know how to report a security incident?

🚩 Common Red Flags to Watch For

Email Red Flags

  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Requests for personal information
  • Misspelled sender domains
  • Generic greetings like "Dear User"
  • Suspicious attachments or links

System Red Flags

  • Unexpected pop-ups or alerts
  • Slow system performance
  • Programs opening automatically
  • Unknown processes running
  • Unusual network activity
