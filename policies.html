Customer Data Protection

Handling Customer Data

Customer data is one of our most valuable assets and must be handled with the utmost care. All employees must follow these guidelines when accessing, storing, or transmitting customer information.

Key Principles

  • Only access customer data when necessary for your job function
  • Never share customer data with unauthorized personnel
  • Do not store customer data on personal devices
  • Always use encrypted channels when transmitting customer data
  • Report any suspected data breach immediately

Customer data includes: names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, ride history, location data, and any other personally identifiable information (PII).

CRM Access Rules

Access to our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system is granted based on role and responsibility.

Access Levels

Read-only: View customer profiles

Standard: Edit customer information

Admin: Full system access

Manager: Team oversight and reporting

Best Practices

  • Log out when leaving your workstation
  • Never share your CRM credentials
  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication

Payment Information Protection

Payment card information is subject to strict PCI-DSS compliance requirements.

Critical Rules

  • Never store full credit card numbers in any system not approved by IT Security
  • Never send payment card details via email or instant messaging
  • Never write down or photograph payment card information
  • Only use company-approved payment processing systems

All payment processing is handled through our PCI-compliant payment gateway.
