Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Visit the internal portal login page and click "Forgot Password". Follow the prompts to receive a password reset link via email. If you encounter issues, contact IT support at ext. 5500.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?

Do not click any links or download attachments. Report the email immediately to security@escooter.com or use the "Report Phishing" button in your email client. Then delete the email from your inbox.

How often do I need to complete security training?

All employees must complete annual security awareness training. You'll receive a notification when your training is due. Additional training may be required for specific roles.

What happens if I lose my company device?

Report lost or stolen devices immediately by calling ext. 911. The security team will remotely wipe the device to protect company and customer data.

Can I use personal devices for work?

Personal devices may only be used for work purposes if enrolled in our Mobile Device Management (MDM) program and approved by IT Security.
